# Change Control / RFC — required sections

1. **RFC Identification** — change number, date, requestor, change type (standard/normal/emergency)
2. **Change Description & Business Justification** — what and why
3. **Risk Assessment** — probability × impact matrix (low/medium/high/critical)
4. **Implementation Plan** — summarised from the MOP, with start/end times
5. **Backout Plan** — how to revert if the change fails
6. **Testing & Validation Plan** — how to verify the change worked
7. **CAB Review Checklist** — items the change advisory board must verify
8. **Post-Implementation Review** — scheduled follow-up date and owner

## Quality bar

Risk assessment must use a probability × impact matrix, not just "low risk".
